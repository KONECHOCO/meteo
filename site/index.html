Aetheria Weather 3D

The weather on a real 3D Earth.

  • Real satellite globe – today's clouds, city lights at night and the true day/night line (NASA imagery).
  • Rain in the next 2 hours in 15-minute steps, plus satellite rain maps.
  • 5 weather models compared – ECMWF, NOAA GFS, DWD ICON, Météo-France, UK Met Office.
  • 14-day forecast, 48-hour detail and a 24-hour meteogram.
  • Pollen, air quality, UV, waves and sea temperature, weather along your route.
  • 225,000 places in 246 countries, searchable offline, in 10 languages.
  • Free with ads, or Aetheria Pro: one purchase, no ads, no subscription.
