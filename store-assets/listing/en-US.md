Aetheria Weather 3D shows the weather on a real 3D Earth, with real, up-to-date weather data.

3D GLOBE WITH NASA IMAGERY
• Real clouds photographed by satellites, city lights at night
• Day and night at the true position of the Sun
• Satellite rain estimate worldwide and a rain map of your area

DETAILED FORECASTS
• Rain in the next 2 hours, in 15-minute steps
• 48 hours hour by hour and 14 days ahead
• Meteogram: temperature, dew point, pressure and rain
• 5 weather models compared: ECMWF, NOAA GFS, DWD ICON, Météo-France, UK Met Office
• Notices for thunderstorms, strong gusts, heavy rain, snow and fog in the next 24 hours

HEALTH AND DAILY LIFE
• Pollen (Europe), air quality, UV index and time to sunburn
• Pressure changes for weather-sensitive people

225,000 PLACES IN 246 COUNTRIES
